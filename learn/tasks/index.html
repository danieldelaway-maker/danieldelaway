The little things.

Ticks and edits save straight to Notion (Work page → Brand Tasks), and changes made in Notion show up here. Tap a project to open it. The number is how many tasks are open (red = something needs you within 2 days). Claude checks Slack and Gmail every 30 minutes and puts what it finds in a yellow Suggested tray; nothing is added until you tap Add. Tasks due more than a week out sit in Later, and people you're waiting on move into your tasks as “Chase” after 5 days. Personal tax and admin live under Life Admin, apart from your work.
