The little things.

Everything lives right here on this page: ticks, edits and new tasks save instantly. Tap a project to open it. Each project has an “Always remember” list at the top for little standing rules (tap one for the details). The number is how many tasks are open (red = something needs you within 2 days). Claude checks Slack and Gmail every 30 minutes and puts what it finds in a yellow Suggested tray; nothing is added until you tap Add. Tasks due more than a week out sit in Later, and people you're waiting on move into your tasks as “Chase” after 5 days. Personal tax and admin live under Life Admin, apart from your work.
