Business costs

From the Expenses → page (Business tab). Only Australian GST can be claimed back, so overseas costs show no GST.

This is a helper for you and Naveen, not tax advice. Foreign amounts use the RBA rate on the invoice/expense date (the ECB rate if the RBA hasn’t got one). GST registered from 1 August 2026, so nothing before that counts.
